From a topology image

Upload a photo, screenshot or export of your low-level topology diagram. Devices, links and interface addresses are read from the image, then hardened configuration files are generated for each device (Cisco IOS, Palo Alto PAN-OS, Fortinet FortiGate or Aruba, based on what the diagram shows).

Every generated file is a draft for review — addresses, routing and credentials must be checked against your standards before applying anything to a live device.
